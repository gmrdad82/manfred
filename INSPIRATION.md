# Inspiration

Where Manfred's ported code comes from. The licences and copyright lines are in NOTICE.

- **Routes** (`crates/motion/src/route.rs`): the arc-length baking of Godot Engine's Curve3D, `scene/resources/curve.cpp` at tag 4.5-stable (commit 876b290332ec6f2e6d173d08162a02aa7e6ca46d), MIT: `_bake_segment3d_even_length`'s recursive even-length subdivision, `_find_interval`'s bisection of the distance table, and `get_closest_offset`'s projection onto the baked polyline. The curve itself is a centripetal Catmull-Rom spline in Hermite form, after Yuksel, Schaefer and Keyser, "Parameterization and applications of Catmull-Rom curves" (2011), evaluated exactly at the baked parameter rather than interpolated between baked points.
- **Noise** (`crates/motion/src/noise.rs`): 1D gradient noise with Perlin's quintic fade (Improved Noise, 2002), two octaves, hashed by the same 64-bit finaliser as `crates/generators/src/noise.rs`.
- **An animated scene file in the window** (`crates/ui/src/animated.rs`): the frame loop of pfx-play's presenter, `crates/play/src/present.rs` at pfx tag v2.2.0 (commit f99be6c4eb9008b8726c339ff29cf7d752582058), MIT: the node palettes sampled from the object's clip, `Draws::models_posed` over the staged instances and glass in draw order, attached lights by `Light::placed_with`, and parented sprays by `Sprays::parents` and `seek_between`.
- **Wireframe** (`crates/ui/src/wire.rs`): three.js at tag r170, MIT. The edge list ports `src/geometries/EdgesGeometry.js`: positions hashed at four decimals, degenerate faces skipped, an edge kept where its two faces' normals part by more than the threshold angle (1°) and every unmatched edge kept as a border, so a flat quad's diagonal is left out. The screen-space line ports the vertex stage of `examples/jsm/lines/LineMaterial.js`: the segment trimmed in front of the camera when one end is behind it, the direction taken in NDC with the aspect undone, the perpendicular offset in pixels scaled by clip w. The edge hasher mixes words like rustc-hash 1.1's FxHasher (MIT or Apache-2.0): rotate left 5, xor, multiply by 0x517cc1b727220a95.
- **Lofts** (`crates/build/src/builtin/loft.rs`): the loft is curvo's `NurbsSurface::try_loft` (https://github.com/mattatz/curvo, version 0.3.2 at commit 6f6a51847a50092507936e9fd5735feedfecb69c, MIT): each column of the sections' points interpolated by `try_interpolate_control_points` (knots by averaging, the basis matrix solved for the control points), with `find_knot_span_index` and `basis_functions` (Piegl and Tiller's A2.1 and A2.2). Unlike curvo, every column shares the stations' own parameters, so the surface has one knot vector in v. The fairing is OpenSubdiv's Catmull-Clark (https://github.com/PixarAnimationStudios/OpenSubdiv, tag v3_7_0, commit 9dab8a47bfbb1388ec8388fe61f5f916e6123f38, Tomorrow Open Source Technology License 1.0, Apache 2.0 with its own trademark section): the face, edge and vertex masks of `opensubdiv/sdc/catmarkScheme.h` and `scheme.h`, the smooth, crease and corner rules, and `opensubdiv/sdc/crease.cpp`'s uniform sharpness decrement and `ComputeFractionalWeightAtVertex` for semi-sharp edges.
